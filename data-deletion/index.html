Delete your Bederf data

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. It takes effect immediately.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Sign in at bederf.co.za/login (with email, Google, Apple or Facebook).
  2. Open Account.
  3. Choose Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

This permanently removes your folio, wishes, photos, friends, imported birthdays, sign-in connections and any celebrations you host. Backups roll off within 14 days.

Signed up with Facebook?

You can also remove Bederf from Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. That stops Facebook sharing anything further with Bederf. To delete the data Bederf already holds, use the steps above, or email us from the address on your account and we'll do it for you within 7 days.

Can't sign in?

Use Forgot password, or email us and we'll verify it's you before deleting. See our Privacy Policy for details.
