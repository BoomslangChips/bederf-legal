Privacy Policy

Bederf ("we") runs Bederf at https://bederf.co.za. This policy explains what personal information we process, why, and your rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Questions or requests: privacy@bederf.co.za.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your actual password).
  • Sign in with Google, Facebook or Apple: your name, email address and that provider's account ID. We never receive your password for those services. Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one.
  • Your folio: anything you choose to add: birthday, sizes, coffee order, favourite stores, wishes, links, product photos and notes.
  • Celebrations: events you host or join, RSVPs, Secret Santa draws, anonymous questions and group chip-in pledges.
  • Friends & imported birthdays: people you add as friends, and, only if you choose to import them, names and birthdays from a contacts file or your Google Contacts. Imported email addresses are stored only as one-way fingerprints so we can tell you which contacts already use Bederf.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and short-lived security logs (IP address) used to stop abuse.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and show your folio to the people you allow (see "Who can see your folio").
  • To fetch product details and photos for links you paste (we contact the store's website on your behalf).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent spam, and fix problems.

We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising. We don't use your data to train AI models.

Who can see your folio

You choose in Edit Folio: Public (findable by name), Link only (anyone you share the link with), or Circle only (people you share a celebration with, and friends). Your birth year is never shown to anyone else. Gift claims ("dibs") on your wishes are always hidden from you.

Who we share it with

  • Sign-in providers (Google, Meta/Facebook, Apple) only when you choose to sign in with them.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs our servers and stores backups.
  • Online stores receive an ordinary web request when we fetch a product link you pasted; no personal information is sent.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your profile, wishes, photos, friends, imported birthdays and sessions straight away; backups roll off within 14 days.

Deleting your data

Signed in, go to Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your folio and everything linked to it, including celebrations you host. If you signed up with Facebook, you can also remove Bederf under Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites; then email privacy@bederf.co.za and we'll delete the account for you.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we process it. Email privacy@bederf.co.za. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforeg.org.za.

Children

Bederf is for people aged 13 and over. Parents can create folios for younger children under their own account.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we'll show a notice in the app before it applies.
